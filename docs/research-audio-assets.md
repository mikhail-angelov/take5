# Audio assets for the demo soundtrack

Research date: 2026-08-18.

## Recommendation

Bundle the three CC0 assets below. CC0 is the least risky choice for an asset
committed to and redistributed with this GitHub repository: Creative Commons says
that it permits copying, modifying, distributing, and performing the work,
including commercially, without permission. It does not require attribution.
([CC0 1.0 Universal deed](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)).

The asset-host pages are the sources for the author, individual asset, download,
and applied licence. As with any public-domain dedication, Creative Commons does
not verify an uploader's copyright status; preserve this record when the files
are added.

| Need | Candidate | Download | Licence and repository use | Attribution |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Background music | [Chill (Loopable)](https://opengameart.org/content/chill-loopable) by Pro Sensory / Alex McCulloch — a low-fi chill track explicitly presented as loopable. | [chillloopable.mp3](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/chillloopable_0.mp3) | The asset page lists **CC0**. It may be copied, modified, and committed/distributed with a commercial or open-source program. | None under CC0. The uploader asks to be named in credits; include `Alex McCulloch — Chill (Loopable), CC0` in a third-party notices file as a courtesy. |
| Pointer click | [Click](https://opengameart.org/content/click) by qubodup — a minimal GUI/mouse click. | [click.wav](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/click.wav) | The asset page lists **CC0**; suitable for bundled commercial redistribution. | None. |
| Typing | [Keyboard Soundpack #1](https://opengameart.org/content/keyboard-soundpack-1-typing-and-single-keystrokes) by unicaegames — contains ten human typing clips at different speeds, seven generated typing clips, and 32 individual keypresses. | [unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1.zip](https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/unicae_games_keyboard_soundpack_1_0.zip) | The asset page lists **CC0**; choose one extracted clip, then commit only that selected source (and its converted render format), not the 8.5 MB source bundle. | None. |

Use quiet gain in the renderer and fade/crossfade at the video boundaries; the
music candidate is already marked loopable, so no bespoke composition is needed.

## Alternatives worth auditioning

These are also CC0 according to their first-party asset pages, but Freesound
requires a free account to download original files, so it has no stable public
raw-file link to include here.

| Need | Candidate | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background music | [Sunset Walk](https://freesound.org/people/KiluaBoy/sounds/436677/) by KiluaBoy | Ambient loop; WAV, stereo, 1:16.799. |
| Click | [Mouse Click.wav](https://freesound.org/people/LilMati/sounds/365648/) by LilMati | UI-oriented WAV, stereo, 0.341 s. |
| Typing | [Keyboard typing](https://freesound.org/people/Breviceps/sounds/447909/) by Breviceps | WAV, stereo, 3.803 s; the author also describes it as public-domain/CC0. |

For a click-only alternative without an account, [Kenney's Interface
Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds) is a 100-file CC0 pack with a
[direct ZIP download](https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/interface-sounds/fa43c1dd4d-1677589452/kenney_interface-sounds.zip).

## Excluded source

Do not bundle Pixabay files for this feature. Its custom licence prohibits
distributing content on a standalone basis. A raw asset committed to a public
GitHub repository creates an avoidable interpretation risk; CC0 has no such
restriction.
